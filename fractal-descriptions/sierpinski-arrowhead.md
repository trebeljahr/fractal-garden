# Sierpinski Arrowhead

One continuous line can approach the same triangular pattern that another construction makes by removing filled triangles. The Sierpinski arrowhead takes the route through that pattern.

## Two bends, one continuous route

Both A and B draw forward. Their rules, `A → B-A-B` and `B → A+B+A`, replace a stroke with three strokes and opposite 60-degree bends.

<figure data-investigation="sierpinski-arrowhead"></figure>

The segment count triples at each rewrite. Alternating orientations keep the pieces joined while preserving triangular gaps.

The limiting path traces the [Sierpinski triangle](/l-system/sierpinski-triangle). The filled-triangle construction makes the missing regions easy to see. The arrowhead makes their connectivity visible.
