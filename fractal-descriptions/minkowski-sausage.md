# Minkowski Sausage

An outward square bump and an inward square bump share each replaced edge. Their areas balance, even while their extra boundary accumulates.

## Length grows while signed area cancels

The rule `F → F+F-F-FF+F+F-F` has eight forward strokes. It spans four unit steps in the old direction.

<figure data-investigation="minkowski-sausage"></figure>

Each stroke shrinks by four when the endpoints stay fixed. Eight strokes at that scale double the boundary length. The similarity dimension is $\log8/\log4=1.5$.

Four copies form a closed island. The matched inward and outward bumps preserve the starting square’s enclosed area. Compare the unbalanced square bumps of the [Quadratic snowflake](/l-system/quadratic-snowflake).
