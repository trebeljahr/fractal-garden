# Koch Anti-Snowflake

Turn the Koch bumps toward the inside of a triangle. The same local four-segment replacement now cuts into the enclosed region.

## Orientation changes the empty space

Every edge becomes four edges one third as long, just as in the [Koch snowflake](/l-system/koch-snowflake). The orientation of the starting boundary decides which side receives each bump.

<figure data-investigation="koch-anti-snowflake"></figure>

The perimeter still multiplies by $4/3$ at every step. That count says nothing about whether the new triangles add or remove enclosed area.

The [Cesàro fractal](/l-system/cesaro-fractal) takes the inward construction further by changing the spike angle.
