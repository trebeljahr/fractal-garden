# Fern 4

A small turn at a growing tip changes the whole plant. In this fern, the continuing tip bends after the side branches return to the stem.

## The bend that stays outside the brackets

The rule is `X → F[+X]F[-X]+X`, with `F → FF`. The two bracketed tips branch away and return. The final X continues after an extra turn.

<figure data-investigation="fern-4"></figure>

Brackets make the side bends temporary. The unbracketed final turn changes the direction inherited by the continuing tip.

This is why moving a turn inside or outside a pair of brackets changes more than one small corner. Its effect repeats through every descendant. Compare the nested junctions in [Fern 1](/l-system/fern-1).
