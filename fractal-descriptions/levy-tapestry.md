# Lévy Tapestry

Start with a square. Replace each side with two equal lines that meet at a right angle, pointing away from the square. The old corners flatten into straight lines, and you get a diamond. Now repeat on all the lines, always folding to the same side as you travel around.<sup><a id="levy-tapestry-ref-1" href="#levy-tapestry-source-1">1</a></sup>

<figure data-construction-player="levy-tapestry"></figure>

Each side of the square has grown into a [Lévy curve](/l-system/levy-curve). So the tapestry is four copies of one curve, each turned a quarter turn from the last.

<figure data-lesson="levy-tapestry"></figure>

From Round 3 on, look for short whiskers pointing into the middle. The pen goes out along a line and comes straight back along it. From then on, the count of strokes runs ahead of the count of different edges.

By Round 12 the outline looks like an octagon, full of lace, with the middle still empty.

The middle stays empty for a simple reason. Each curve bulges a long way out from its side, up to one full side length. It only ever reaches about a quarter of a side length back in. So no line ever enters the central square, half a side wide.<sup><a id="levy-tapestry-ref-2" href="#levy-tapestry-source-2">2</a></sup>
