# Mosely Snowflake

Divide a cube into 27 equal cubes and remove the eight corner cubes. Nineteen remain. Repeat inside each remaining cube.

An alternate version removes the center cube too, leaving eighteen cubes each time.

<figure data-construction-player="mosely-snowflake"></figure>

Each step leaves more cubes, but less material. Keeping the center cube leaves $19/27$ of the previous volume. Removing it leaves $18/27$.

Look toward one corner of a cube. Its outline is a hexagon. Keep the center cube at each step, and the smaller cubes overlap in this view. Their combined shadow approaches a filled [Koch snowflake](/l-system/koch-snowflake) as you repeat the construction.<sup><a id="mosely-snowflake-ref-1" href="#mosely-snowflake-source-1">1</a></sup>

Remove the center cube too, and holes appear in the shadow. You can see all the way through some parts.

<figure data-article-images="mosely-koch"></figure>

You can build a model with business cards. Fold six cards into a cube, then tuck their flaps into neighboring cubes. The USC project called for about 49,000 cards.<sup><a id="mosely-snowflake-ref-2" href="#mosely-snowflake-source-2">2</a></sup>
