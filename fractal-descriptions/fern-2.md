# Fern 2

Long stems and small paired leaflets follow different rules in this fern. Two kinds of growing tip divide those jobs.

## Separate rules for stems and leaflets

The Y rule, `Y → YFX[+Y][-Y]`, creates the branching framework. The X rule, `X → X[-FFF][+FFF]FX`, adds paired leaflets.

<figure data-investigation="fern-2"></figure>

Existing F strokes remain unchanged. Growth comes from expanding X and Y, unlike [Fern 1](/l-system/fern-1), where each F also doubles.

This distinction changes how old stems compare with new growth. The branching topology comes from the word. The turn angle controls how widely that structure spreads.
