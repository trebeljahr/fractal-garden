# Terdragon

Three folded segments replace each old segment. Their 120-degree turns create triangular and hexagonal patterns rather than the square rhythm of the Dragon curve.

## Three copies and a rotating chord

The rule `F → F+F-F` triples the segment count. The endpoint chord grows by $\sqrt{3}$ and rotates by 30 degrees before rescaling.

<figure data-investigation="terdragon"></figure>

Three copies at scale $1/\sqrt{3}$ give similarity dimension 2. The limiting curve fills a region, while each displayed stage remains a finite path.

The [Dragon curve](/l-system/dragon-curve) uses two pieces and right-angle turns. The [Twindragon](/l-system/twindragon) closes two such paths into a tile.
