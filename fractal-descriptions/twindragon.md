# Twindragon

Two Dragon curves meet at both endpoints. One travels out, the other returns, enclosing the Twindragon tile.

## A half-turn joins the two folds

Take a Dragon path and rotate a second copy by 180 degrees around the midpoint of its endpoint chord. The second copy reverses the endpoints.

<figure data-investigation="twindragon"></figure>

Each half doubles its strokes at every replacement. The two-colored trace separates the folds even when their smaller features become crowded.

Translated copies of the limiting tile cover the plane. Its interior has positive area, so its dimension is 2. Its rough boundary is a different object with a different dimension. Compare the three-part [Terdragon](/l-system/terdragon).
