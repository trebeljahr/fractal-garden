# Quadratic Snowflake

Square bumps replace the triangular bumps of the Koch snowflake. Each edge gains a detour around three sides of a smaller square.

## Five strokes across a three-stroke span

The replacement `F → F+F-F-F+F` has five strokes and right-angle turns. It ends three unit lengths along the old direction.

<figure data-investigation="quadratic-snowflake"></figure>

Keeping the original endpoint spacing shrinks each new segment by three. The perimeter multiplies by $5/3$ per replacement.

The same bump idea extends to square faces in the [quadratic Koch surface](/quadratic-koch-3d). There, a central square becomes the exposed walls of a small cube.
